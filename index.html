📖 Official Ludo Rules

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to move all four of your colored tokens from your yard, around the track, and into your home center triangle.

🎲 Rolling a Six (6)

  • Rolling a 6 unlocks a token from your yard onto your starting cell.
  • Alternatively, you can choose to advance an existing token by 6 steps.
  • Rolling a 6 awards a bonus roll! (Max 3 consecutive sixes).

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Zones

  • Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to their yard!
  • Capturing an opponent's token awards an immediate bonus turn.
  • Cells marked with a Star (★) and colored start cells are Safe Zones where tokens cannot be captured. Multiple tokens can share these cells safely.

🏆 Winning the Match

Tokens enter their dedicated colored home corridor towards the center. An exact roll is required to reach the Home Goal. The first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins!
